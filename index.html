Why now

Email got more valuable in 2026. It also got a lot harder.

Paid traffic is rented. Your list is owned. But the rules for reaching an inbox, and for proving what email earns, changed. Most brands and agencies never caught up.

Gmail now rejects, not just filters

Full enforcement of bulk-sender rules since November 2025. Unauthenticated mail can be refused at the server.

Outlook joined in

Since May 2025, Microsoft rejects non-compliant mail from domains sending over 5,000 a day.

Open rates stopped meaning much

Apple's Mail Privacy Protection inflates more than half of all opens. Reports built on opens are guessing.

Attribution isn't proof

Klaviyo itself says attribution doesn't prove causation. A holdout test does.

Generic AI costs trust

Klaviyo's 2026 research: only 13% of consumers fully trust AI. Emails built on your own customer data still earn.

Every re-buy you miss, you pay for twice

When a customer runs out and nothing reaches them, the reorder goes to whoever shows up in their feed next.
